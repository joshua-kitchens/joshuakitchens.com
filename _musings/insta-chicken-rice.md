<p>[recipe title="Instant Pot Chicken and Rice Soup" servings="6" time="1.5 hr" difficulty="easy" description="This recipe in experimentation in making a quick and creamy chicken and rice soup in an instant pot." image="https://www.joshuakitchens.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/IMG_1238.jpg"][recipe-notes]This recipe in experimentation in making a quick and creamy chicken and rice soup in an instant pot. This recipe has been adapted and modified from Betty Crocker:<br />
Original Instant Pot Chicken and Wild Rice Soup<br />
[/recipe-notes][recipe-ingredients]- 20 oz Chicken Thighs- 2 cups Onion- 8 oz Mushrooms- ½ cup Celery- ½ cup Rice- 1 cup Heavy Cream- 2 tbsp Corn Starch- Salt and Pepper- 1/4 tsp thyme[/recipe-ingredients][recipe-directions]</p>
<p>Season chicken thighs with salt and pepper<br />
Set instapot to saute, melt butter or heat oil of choice, until hot. Cook chicken on each side for 5 - 6 minute per side. Leaving residual fond in instapot and transfer cooked chicken to cutting board.<br />
Using tongs, transfer chicken to a cutting board and chop.<br />
While instapot is still set to saute, add onions, mushrooms, season with salt and pepper and cook 4 - 5 minutes or until softened.<br />
Add carrots and celery, and continue cooking for 4 - 5 minutes until all vegetables are softened.<br />
Add chicken broth; heat to simmering, scraping the bottom of the bowl with a wooden spoon to remove browned bits. Stir in rice, thyme and chopped chicken. Select CANCEL to turn off suate function.<br />
Secure lid; set pressure valve to SEALING. Select MANUAL; cook on high pressure 45 minutes. Set pressure valve to VENTING to quick-release pressure or let naturally release.<br />
Select SAUTE; adjust to normal. Stir in chopped chicken; heat to boiling. In 2-cup measuring cup, beat whipping cream and cornstarch together. Stir into mixture in instapot; cook 2 to 4 minutes, stirring frequently, until slightly thickened. Top with thyme leaves.</p>
