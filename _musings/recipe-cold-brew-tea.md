<p>[recipe title="Cold Brew Tea" servings="6" time="8 - 12 hr" difficulty="easy" description="This recipe is a work in progress for cold brew tea. You could sweeten this after brewing with simple sugar syrup. I’m currently working on a honey simple syrup." image="https://www.joshuakitchens.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2214-e1569603906666.jpg"][recipe-notes] </p>
<p>Recipe based off of  https://cookieandkate.com/cold-brew-iced-tea/ </p>
<p>[/recipe-notes]</p>
<p>[recipe-ingredients] </p>
<p>1 teaspoon of loose leaf tea per 6 -8 oz of water or one tea bag per 12 - 16 oz of water. Water</p>
<p>[/recipe-ingredients]</p>
<p>[recipe-directions] </p>
<p>Measure out the appropriate amount of tea for your pitcher or brewing vesselPour water and tea into the brewing vesselLet sit covered in the refrigerator for 6 - 12 hoursStrain tea and serve</p>
<p>[/recipe-directions][/recipe]</p>
