<p>Apparently there are two versions of the video of Flannery O'Connor with you backwards walking chicken. The Pathe News Reel company created two versions of this news reel. One entitled "Unique Chicken Goes in Reverse" appeared to be for the american market. This video is available without sound in the Flannery O'Connor Collection in the Ina Dillard Russell Library, Special Collections. The other entitled "Do You Reverse" seems to have been created for the British and possibly European Market. You can see it below as released from British Pathe.</p>
