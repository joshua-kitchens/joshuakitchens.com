This recipe is an experiment in making a quick and creamy chicken and rice soup in an Instant Pot.

**Serves:** 6 · **Time:** 1.5 hr · **Difficulty:** Easy

## Notes

This recipe has been adapted and modified from Betty Crocker's Instant Pot Chicken and Wild Rice Soup.

## Ingredients

- 20 oz chicken thighs
- 2 cups onion
- 8 oz mushrooms
- ½ cup celery
- ½ cup rice
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 2 tbsp corn starch
- Salt and pepper
- ¼ tsp thyme

## Directions

1. Season chicken thighs with salt and pepper.
2. Set the Instant Pot to sauté and melt butter or heat oil of choice until hot. Cook chicken for 5–6 minutes per side, leaving the residual fond in the pot.
3. Using tongs, transfer chicken to a cutting board and chop.
4. While the Instant Pot is still set to sauté, add onions and mushrooms, season with salt and pepper, and cook 4–5 minutes or until softened.
5. Add carrots and celery, and continue cooking for 4–5 minutes until all vegetables are softened.
6. Add chicken broth; heat to simmering, scraping the bottom of the pot with a wooden spoon to remove browned bits. Stir in rice, thyme, and chopped chicken. Select CANCEL to turn off the sauté function.
7. Secure lid; set pressure valve to SEALING. Select MANUAL; cook on high pressure 45 minutes. Set pressure valve to VENTING to quick-release pressure, or let it release naturally.
8. Select SAUTÉ; adjust to normal. Stir in chopped chicken; heat to boiling. In a 2-cup measuring cup, beat whipping cream and cornstarch together. Stir into the soup; cook 2 to 4 minutes, stirring frequently, until slightly thickened. Top with thyme leaves.
